Recover the sensor readings

Undo a sensor’s memory fault: design your solution on paper, then bring it to life with NumPy.

20 minutes on paper + 25 minutes coding
Allow extra time for setup and submission.

Before you start

  • Have a laptop, paper and a pen. Use your phone for paper photos; you can take or choose individual photos if a continuous camera setup is inconvenient.
  • Groups of 1–3 form automatically. Three waiting students start together; otherwise, after about one minute, you start alone or with one partner. You cannot choose partners on this page.
  • Your group stays fixed once the room is created. JupyterLab can take a few more minutes to open.

Sign in with your CMU Google account to join automatically. Any @cmu.edu address or CMU subdomain, such as @andrew.cmu.edu, is eligible. Previously invited accounts can also sign in.
